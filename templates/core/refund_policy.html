Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: August 16, 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to all course purchases made on edufixlearning.com by EDUFIX LEARNING INSTITUTION LLP ("Edufix Learning Academy", "Edufix", "we", "us", or "our"). We want you to be confident in your enrollment, which is why eligible courses come with a money-back guarantee as described below.

1. Money-Back Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with a course, you may request a full refund, provided your request is made before you start (access) the 3rd class of the course, as explained below.

2. Eligibility for Refund

  • You have accessed or completed no more than the first 2 classes of the course.
  • The refund request is made before you open or start the 3rd class.
  • The course was purchased directly on edufixlearning.com.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

  • Refund requests made after the 3rd class of the course has been accessed or started.
  • Promotional, discounted, or bundled purchases explicitly marked as "non-refundable" at checkout, if any.
  • Cases of suspected misuse, such as sharing account access or course content with others.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us with your registered email address, the course name, and the order/payment reference number via:

Email: info@edufixlearning.com
WhatsApp / Phone: +91 7034893345

5. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds will be processed to your original payment method within 7-10 business days. The exact time for the amount to reflect in your account may vary depending on your bank or payment provider.

6. Course Access After a Refund

Once a refund is approved and processed, your access to the course content will be revoked.

7. Cancellation Before Course Completion

You may cancel your enrollment at any time by contacting us. If the cancellation is made before you start the 3rd class, it will be treated as a refund request under this Policy.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Effective Date."
